### Full Description (4000 characters max)
```text
The PM e-Vidya Portal mobile application is the official enterprise platform developed by the National Instructional Media Institute (NIMI), an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE), Government of India.

Designed to streamline and monitor the production of high-quality digital learning content across national vocational trades, the PM e-Vidya application empowers administrators, subject matter experts, and media production teams to track, verify, and approve instructional video assets in real-time.

KEY FEATURES:

1. OFFICIAL COMMUNICATIONS & APPROVAL LETTERS
Generate and manage standardized SME Deputation Letters, Official Communications, and Work Completion Certificates with official institutional formatting.

2. SME & WORKFLOW MANAGEMENT
Track Subject Matter Expert (SME) engagements, session attendance, topic coverage, and honorarium calculations aligned with NCERT and Ministry guidelines.

3. PRODUCTION & VIDEO TRACKER
Monitor video recording, editing, review, and finalization milestones across all ITI trade curricula. Real-time status indicators ensure complete visibility over content readiness.

4. REAL-TIME SYNCHRONIZATION
Seamless bidirectional synchronization between the Web Portal and Android application powered by low-latency server events and authoritative Neon PostgreSQL cloud data.

5. ENTERPRISE ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL
Granular permissions strictly enforced across Administrator, Team Member, Cameraman, Editor, and Official Viewer roles.

6. DATA INTEGRITY & INTERNET-ONLY SAFETY
PM e-Vidya operates in verified internet-only mode, blocking unauthenticated offline write queues and ensuring financial and government records are always authoritative.

For technical assistance or official inquiries, please contact NIMI Chennai at chennai-nimi@nic.in or visit https://pmevidyanimi.online.
```

## 6. App Access Instructions for Google Reviewers

Because PM e-Vidya enforces strict role-based authentication, the Google Play review team requires demonstration credentials:

- **Review Access Option:** "All or some functionality is restricted (e.g. login credentials required)"
- **Username / Email:** `admin@pmevidya.internal` (or designated reviewer account)
- **Password:** Provided in secure Google Play Console Review Notes field.
- **Login Instructions:**
  1. Open the application; the splash screen navigates to the secure login gateway.
  2. Enter the provided reviewer credentials and click **Sign In**.
  3. The reviewer is granted access to the Dashboard, SME Work Entries, Official Communications, and Video Production Tracker.
  4. Note that all test submissions are isolated and do not alter production records.
